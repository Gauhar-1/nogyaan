# Brand Film Screenplay: QuickbiteQr

* **Product:** Waiter Bell, a new QuickbiteQr feature that lets diners call a waiter instantly from their phone by scanning the table QR code
* **Campaign Goal:** Make people aware of the new Waiter Bell feature and drive restaurants and diners to try it
* **Framework Preset:** indian-d2c-story

## Core Campaign Premise
A comedy of everyday dining chaos. In a packed Saturday-night Indian restaurant, every table has its own desperate, loving, ridiculous way of trying to get the waiter's attention: finger clicks, dupatta waves, kids shouting 'Bhaiyaaa!', dramatic eye-contact stares. The waiter is a blur of motion who never sees any of it. Then one calm table scans the QR and taps the Waiter Bell. The waiter appears in seconds, smiling, while the rest of the room is still flailing. The truth underneath: nobody wants to shout for care, they just want to be noticed. QuickbiteQr's Waiter Bell turns the noise into a quiet tap.

### Scene 01: The Uncle Who Tries Everything (8s)
- **Act:** Act I — Setup
- **Camera:** 35mm f/1.8 · Eye-level medium shot of the Sharma family table, Sharma ji centre-frame, with the busy hall softly blurred behind · Slow, steady dolly-in from medium shot to a tighter medium close-up on Sharma ji, then a slight drift right to catch Anaya lifting her phone
- **Physical Blocking:** Sharma ji taps a steel spoon against his empty steel glass three times, then thrusts one arm into the air, snaps his fingers, coughs loudly into his fist and waves broadly as Ravi passes behind him with a loaded tray without turning his head. Sunita leans in and whispers sharply with a sideways glance at the neighbouring tables, Rohan slides lower behind the laminated menu until only his eyes show, and Sharma ji's raised arm slowly sags until the spoon hangs limp from his fingers. Anaya quietly lifts her phone from her lap as the camera drifts toward her.
- **On-Camera Dialogue (Lip-Sync):** "SHARMA JI: "Ae bhaiya, suniye zara!" | SUNITA (whispering): "Arre, shor mat karo, sab dekh rahe hain.""
- **SFX & Ambience:** Busy restaurant ambience with clinking plates, overlapping Hindi chatter and a distant kitchen clatter. Steel spoon ting-ting-ting on the glass, a sharp finger snap, a loud cough, and the rustle of a laminated menu card.
- **Editorial Cut Note:** Hard cut on the chime sound at the start of Scene 2. Keep the room ambience running underneath the cut.

- **Act:** Act II — Catalyst
- **Camera:** 50mm f/1.4 · Intimate eye-level close two-shot of Anaya and Sharma ji across the table, phone screen and QR stand in the lower third · Slow push-in from the two-shot to a tight close-up on Anaya's thumb hovering over the glowing Waiter Bell button on the phone screen
- **Physical Blocking:** Sharma ji lifts the spoon for one last desperate try, but Anaya reaches across and gently catches his wrist, shaking her head with a small grin. She turns her phone toward him and the glowing marigold-gold Waiter Bell button lights up his raised eyebrows. Sunita and Rohan lean in over the screen, Rohan lowering the menu for the first time, while the camera pushes in until Anaya's thumb hovers over the button, then taps it, and the screen slides up 'Waiter notified' with a soft chime.
- **On-Camera Dialogue (Lip-Sync):** "ANAYA: "Papa, bas ek tap." | SHARMA JI: "Phone se waiter aayega? Mazaak hai kya?""
- **SFX & Ambience:** Restaurant ambience dips slightly to a hush. A soft rustle of Anaya's cardigan sleeve, a gentle tap on the glass screen, then a single warm bell chime and a quiet notification swoosh as 'Waiter notified' slides up.
- **Editorial Cut Note:** Cut on the first soft chime of the Waiter Bell into the wide hall shot of Scene 3. Let the chime ring over the cut.

### Scene 03: The Whole Hall Watches (8s)
- **Act:** Act III — Twist
- **Camera:** 24mm f/2.8 · Low-to-eye-level wide shot that begins tight on the QR stand and empty steel glass, and ends as a full dining-hall wide with neighbouring tables in the foreground · Smooth continuous pull-back and slow rise from the glowing QR stand on the family table, sweeping through the aisle to reveal the hall and neighbouring tables, ending on a wide shot with Ravi arriving at the family table in the background
- **Physical Blocking:** The camera pulls back from the glowing QR stand and the empty steel glass, rising over the table edge as the chime fades, and sweeps into the aisle where Ravi sets down a tray and walks calmly and directly toward the Sharma table. In the foreground the aunty freezes with her orange dupatta still raised, the couple break their eye-contact duel mid-stare, and the little boy stops with his mouth open mid-shout, all turning their heads to follow Ravi. One by one they lower their hands and their eyes drift down to the QR stands on their own tables as Ravi arrives behind them at the family table.
- **On-Camera Dialogue (Lip-Sync):** "KID (mid-shout, cut off): "Bhaiyaaa—""
- **SFX & Ambience:** The Waiter Bell chime rings out clean and warm, then fades. The hall's noise falls away in stages to a held hush: chatter drops, clinking plates pause, a ceiling fan hums softly. Ravi's calm footsteps on the wooden floor, the soft swish of the aunty's dupatta settling, and a faint breath from the kid.
- **Editorial Cut Note:** Let the final frozen beat of lowered hands and downward glances hold for half a second, then cut to Scene 4 on Sharma ji's first bite of dal.

### Scene 04: Bhaiya, Bell Dabao (8s)
- **Act:** Act IV — Climax
- **Camera:** 35mm f/1.8 · Eye-level medium shot over Sharma ji's shoulder, framing the neighbouring uncle's table and his QR stand in the background · Slow dolly-in and a gentle drift right from Sharma ji's table to the neighbouring uncle's table, ending in a tight close-up on the uncle's hand holding his phone to the QR stand as the Waiter Bell icon glows on screen
- **Physical Blocking:** Sharma ji takes a satisfied first spoonful of steaming dal, savours it with closed eyes, then turns his whole body toward the neighbouring uncle's table and raises his spoon like a pointer with a cheeky grin. The neighbouring uncle, who is still drumming his fingers on the white tablecloth, looks across, then down at the QR stand in front of him, and shrugs with one shoulder. He lifts his phone and scans the QR code as the camera dollies in and drifts right, ending in a tight close-up on his hand as the marigold-gold Waiter Bell icon glows on the screen.
- **On-Camera Dialogue (Lip-Sync):** "SHARMA JI (grinning, spoon raised): "Bhaiya, QR scan karo, bell dabao. Kasam se, kaam kar jaata hai.""

- **On-Screen Graphic:** End card in post: QuickbiteQr logo with a glowing Waiter Bell icon, tagline "Ab bulana nahi padta. Bas ek tap.", final line "Aaj hi try karo."
- **Editorial Cut Note:** Cut to a clean end card on a warm terracotta background right as the Waiter Bell icon glows on the uncle's phone. Add the logo, the tagline and the final CTA line in post.
